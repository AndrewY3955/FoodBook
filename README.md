# CookistryBook

**Your recipes, your pantry, your week — in one place.**

CookistryBook is a recipe app for home cooks. Keep every recipe you love in a
shared cookbook, see what you can make with the food you already have, plan
the week's meals, and turn the plan into a shopping list. It works the same on
a phone, a tablet and a computer.

Open it here: **https://andrewy3955.github.io/FoodBook/**

---

## What you can do

### Recipes
- Save recipes with photos, ingredients, steps, serving size, prep and cook
  time, cuisine, diet labels (vegetarian, gluten-free and so on) and a
  nutrition label.
- Sort recipes into **albums** such as "Weeknight Dinners" or "Holiday
  Baking". Every cookbook starts with a "General" album. You can add, rename
  and delete albums at any time. Deleting an album never deletes its recipes.
- Search, filter by meal, season, cuisine or diet, and switch between a
  picture grid and a simple list.
- Scale a recipe up or down and the ingredient amounts change with it.
- Share a recipe with everyone on **Discover**, or keep it private.

### Cook
- **Start cooking** opens one step at a time in big text, with built-in timers.
- Make your own copy of any recipe and change it as you like.

### Pantry
- Tell the app what you have at home and how much.
- It ranks your recipes by how many ingredients you already have, and shows
  which ones you can make right now.

### Plan
- Put meals on the calendar for the week and set how many people you are
  cooking for.

### Shopping
- Add a whole recipe or the whole week's plan to your shopping list.
- Only what your pantry is missing is added. Tick items off as you shop, then
  clear the ticked ones with one tap.

### Cookbooks and sharing
- You can belong to as many cookbooks as you like and switch between them.
- Invite family or friends with a one-time code. Everyone in a cookbook sees
  the same recipes, pantry, plan and list.
- If you leave a cookbook and you were the only member, the cookbook is
  deleted. The owner can also delete a cookbook for good after confirming.
  This cannot be undone.

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** with your email and a password. Confirm it from the
   email we send you. If it doesn't arrive, tap *Resend* (available again
   after 60 seconds).
2. **Start a cookbook**, or join one with an invitation code.
3. **Add a recipe** from the Recipes tab with the *Add recipe* button.
4. **Fill your pantry** on the Pantry tab with the *Add to pantry* button.
5. **Plan and shop**: add meals on the Plan tab, then open Shopping.

### The five tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | Recipes other people have shared |
| **Recipes** | Your cookbook, organized in albums |
| **Plan** | Your meals for the week |
| **Shopping** | What to buy, with a tick-off list |
| **Pantry** | What you have, and what you can cook with it |

Settings (the gear at the top) holds your cookbook name, members and
invitations, and your account.

---

## Your account and privacy

- Changing your password requires confirming by email.
- **Sign out** and **Delete account** are in Settings, under Account. Deleting
  your account removes your recipes, photos, pantry and plans for good.
- You only ever see cookbooks you belong to. Recipes are private unless you
  choose to share them to Discover.

## Made for everyone

Large tap targets, clear text labels on every important button, support for
larger text sizes, screen readers and reduced motion, and a dark theme.
